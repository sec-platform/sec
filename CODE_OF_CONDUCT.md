# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation should be respectful, technically honest, and focused on improving the work. Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- distinguish verified facts, inferences, proposals, and unknowns;
- accept correction and give actionable review feedback;
- respect privacy, authorship, licensing, and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, reject changes, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, correction, and the safety of affected participants.

Use GitHub's built-in content-reporting tools for abusive content or account behavior. Report security vulnerabilities through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish another person's private information while reporting conduct.
